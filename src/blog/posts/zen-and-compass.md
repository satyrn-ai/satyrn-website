Welcome to Satyrn where we care about sharing knowledge and empowering you
on your journey with "trusted and transparent" AI. We've learned some valuable
lessons during our time in the Python community working with the Python
language and want to share those lessons with you.

<!-- more -->

## Who is Satyrn

It's an ecosystem of people who come from different domains and backgrounds.
As a system of humans working together, we share knowledge to build trust and
transparency in AI systems. So, how do we do this? It starts with building
a solid foundation for how we work together (Team Compass) and sharing the
learnings we've gained along the way as we work with AI (Satyrn Zen).

## What is a Team Compass

The "Team Compass" concept started at Project Jupyter when we were building
JupyterHub and Binder. Jupyter was facing meteoric growth in the use of notebooks for data science,
scientific computing, and education. We wanted to build a community around our work
to serve notebooks at scale in the cloud and on-premises. We recognized that
many people with different backgrounds and expertise helped us work more effectively
and creatively. The "Team Compass" began as a way to share how we work together to
meet our goals and communicate effectively.

More than a set of documents, the Satyrn [**Team Compass**](https://satyrn-ai.github.io/team-compass/)
captures how we work, how we collaborate, and how we make decisions.
It's a living document that we update as the organization grows and evolves.
The most important thing about the Team Compass is that it shares with you
how to get follow our work, get involved, and contribute.

As a living document, the Team Compass is constantly evolving and improving.
We welcome contributions from anyone who wants to help us shape the future of AI.

## What is Satyrn Zen

Learning, creating, and sharing openly are some of Satyrn's principles as
we work toward more "trusted and transparent" AI systems.
[**Satyrn Zen**](https://satyrn-ai.github.io/satyrn-zen/) is our
open book of learning documents, videos, and other resources that we share
with the community.

Satyrn Zen shares documents in three distinct categories:

- **Learn**: reference documents and guides for understanding the concepts of
  AI tools and technologies and its impact on society.
- **Create**: "How-to" guides and tutorials for using AI tools and technologies.
- **Share**: Links to resources such as talks, blog posts, videos to share work
  and ideas within the Satyrn community and beyond.

Each category, learn, create, and share, is composed of two document collections:

- content written by the Satyrn team and community members
- community-curated content from external sources that fit our Satyrn's mission

We welcome contributions from anyone who wants to contribute to Satyrn Zen.
Please file an issue on content that you would like to see and PRs are welcome too.
We want our information to be accessible and serve a range of audiences, from
individuals learning about AI to organizations looking to explore cutting-edge
AI technologies. To be truly accessible, we encourage content written *by
humans for humans*.

## What's next

We're taking this journey and exploring together. We hope you will join us and
support our mission to **make AI systems more accessible, trusted, and humane for
everyone**.
