# Carol Willing

Carol cares deeply about how technology impacts people and society.
She approaches life with an insatiable appetite for learning and a passion
for sharing her knowledge with others and learning alongside them.
As a three-time Python Steering Council member including serving on the inaugural
council, a Python core team member, and Project Jupyter core contributor, her
dedication to the Python language and community can be felt far and wide.
Carol's [keynotes](https://speakerdeck.com/willingc) and her
[blog](https://willingconsulting.com) share more about her expertise.
