We want to start a conversation in the world of Python about AI Our Way and Laptop AI,
build a network using old-school web technology, and amplify all the people doing good,
innovative work.

## Where to Find Us

### Mastodon

Follow us on [Mastodon](https://social.satyrn.ai/explore) for updates, discussions,
and links to interesting work in the Python AI community.

### YouTube

Watch talks, demos, and conversations about local-first AI, small models, and what it means to do AI Our Way.

Coming soon — stay tuned.

### LinkedIn

Connect with us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/satyrn-ai/home/)
for professional updates, community news, and opportunities to get involved.

## Coming Soon

We're planning more ways to connect and build community:

- **RSS-powered webring** — a curated network of Python AI practitioners, shareable and discoverable through RSS feeds.
- **More platforms** — we're exploring where else to meet the community where they are.

We want to hear from you: **how can we build a trusted community?** Share your thoughts on [Discord](https://discord.gg/v4uejrA88f) or [get involved](../get-involved.md) — we're listening.
